2. Problem Statement & Approach

The Business Problem

A SaaS subscription company with 20,000 customers and $10.5M in Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) was experiencing significant customer churn. The company had no systematic way to:

  • Identify which customers are about to cancel their subscription
  • Understand why customers are leaving (pricing? support? product usage?)
  • Quantify how much revenue is at risk in dollar terms
  • Prioritize which accounts the Customer Success team should call first
The Cost of Doing Nothing: Without intervention, the company stands to lose $274,093 every month ($3.3M annually) in expected revenue, and $1.07M ARR is directly exposed through 2,484 high-risk accounts.

Our Approach (Step by Step)

1
Data Collection & Validation: Collected 615,628 records across 4 datasets. Validated schema, nulls, duplicates, referential integrity.
2
SQL Analytics & EDA: Wrote 10 SQL scripts + 15+ visualizations revealing churn patterns by plan, tenure, region.
3
Statistical Hypothesis Testing: Z-tests, T-tests, Chi-squared, ANOVA. All key churn drivers showed p < 0.001.
4
Feature Engineering: Engineered 27 predictive features with temporal cutoff at June 30, 2025 to prevent data leakage.
5
ML Model Training: Trained Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting. Best: GBM with 0.75 ROC-AUC.
6
Risk Scoring & Deliverables: Converted probabilities into 0-100 risk scores, 4 tiers, root cause identification, and revenue-at-risk calculations.
7
Power BI Dashboard: Built 3-page interactive dashboard: Executive Overview -> Churn Drivers -> Action Center.

4. Stakeholder Business Questions -- Answered

Q1: How much churn is happening?
Our overall churn rate is 33.0% (6,603 out of 20,000 customers). One in 3 customers has either churned or is highly likely to churn. The expected monthly revenue loss is $274,093/month, translating to $3.29M annually.
Q2: Which customer segments churn the most?
Basic Monthly subscribers are the highest-churning segment (avg risk 35.2, 2,393 churned). Monthly billing customers churn at 37.5% vs 22.9% for Annual. By region, North America (1,610) and Europe (1,131) have the highest absolute churn volumes.
Q3: What behavioral factors are associated with churn?
Top 5 drivers ranked by revenue impact: (1) Payment Failure -- $204,014/mo (74.4%), (2) Poor Support -- $38,788/mo (14.2%), (3) General Attrition -- $16,514/mo (6.0%), (4) Monthly Contract Friction -- $14,246/mo (5.2%), (5) Declining Usage -- $532/mo (0.2%).
Q4: How does churn vary by subscription plan?
Basic: Highest churn count (2,393) -- lower price = less switching cost. Pro: Moderate (1,159) -- deeper feature engagement aids retention. Enterprise: Lowest rate (350) -- but each churner = $199/mo loss, making them the costliest individual losses.
Q5: How does tenure affect churn?
Risk increases with tenure: 0-6 months (avg risk 26.1) -> 25-30 months (avg risk 37.3). Longer-tenured customers have experienced more cumulative payment failures and support issues. The "fatigue zone" is at 19+ months.
Q6: Does payment behavior influence churn?
Absolutely -- it is the single strongest predictor. Accounts with >=1 failed payment: 29.6% churn rate (risk 41.6). Zero failures: 5.2% churn rate (risk 20.9). That is a 5.7× difference. Failed payments = $204,014 monthly expected loss (74.4% of all loss).
Q7: Which customers are currently at high risk?
2,484 accounts (12.4%) are High or Critical: 270 Critical (83.6% churn prob, need 48-hour outreach) and 2,214 High (67.8%, need proactive campaign). Top 5 highest-risk are Enterprise Monthly with payment failures and CSAT below 2.0.
Q8: What is the potential revenue at risk?
$88,866 MRR ($1.07M ARR) is tied to High/Critical accounts. Probability-weighted expected loss = $274,093/month ($3.29M annually). Saving 30% of at-risk accounts protects approximately $320,000+ annually.
Q9: Which customers should the business prioritize?
Priority by "Expected Monthly Loss" ranking: Tier 1 -- 270 Critical accounts -> Personal phone call by Sr. CSM this week. Tier 2 -- 2,214 High accounts -> Personalized email + discount offer within 2 weeks. Tier 3 -- 7,416 Medium -> Automated health check emails.
Q10: What actions could reduce churn?
4 highest-ROI interventions: (1) Payment Dunning Automation -- save $61K/mo, (2) Monthly->Annual Conversion -- save $30.5K/mo, (3) CSAT Escalation -- save $9.7K/mo, (4) Usage Re-Engagement -- save $213/mo. Total projected savings: $1.22M/year.

Implementation Priority Roadmap

Week 1-2 Quick Win: Start calling 270 Critical accounts immediately using high_risk_retention_list.csv. Assign 10-15 accounts per CSM per day.
Week 2-4 Payment Recovery: Set up automated dunning system: auto-retry at Days 3, 5, 7 + pre-expiry card update reminders. Expected recovery: 30%+ of failed payments.
Month 2 Annual Conversion: Launch Monthly->Annual campaign: "2 months free" for Annual upgrade. 15% conversion = 2,085 accounts locked in for 12 months.
Month 3 Support & Engagement: Auto-escalate tickets with CSAT <= 2 to Senior Support within 4 hours. Deploy in-app product walkthroughs for declining-usage accounts.
